# Methodology

## Synthetic cohort design

Pairing grade `g` in year `t` with grade `g-1` in year `t-1` approximates within-student growth at the cohort level without requiring longitudinal student-level data. Rationale, assumptions, and known biases to be documented here.

## Choice of baseline SD vs. pooled SD

Effect sizes use the prior-year `(g-1, t-1)` SD as the denominator rather than a pooled cross-year or cross-grade SD. Justification for this choice to be documented here.

## Treatment of the COVID gap (2020)

Year-grade cells that depend on 2020 MAP data are unrecoverable because of the cancelled spring 2020 administration. Specific affected cohorts and the handling strategy to be documented here.

## Sensitivity to vertical-scale assumptions

Missouri MAP scale scores are vertically equated across grades 3–8. Implications for the comparability of growth estimates across grades — and how robust the conversion factors are to plausible departures from perfect vertical equating — to be documented here.
